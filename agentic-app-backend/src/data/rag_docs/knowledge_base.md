# Company Knowledge Base

This document contains official company policies used by the support assistant to answer customer questions. Each section below is self-contained so it can be chunked and embedded independently for retrieval.

## Refund Policy

- Customers are eligible for a full refund if the request is made within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Refunds are only issued to the original payment method used at checkout. We cannot refund to a different card, account, or via cash.
- To request a refund, the customer must provide the order ID and a reason for the refund.
- Once a refund is approved, it takes **5–7 business days** to appear on the customer's statement, depending on their bank or card issuer.
- Digital products, gift cards, and items marked "Final Sale" are **not eligible** for refunds.
- If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is the wrong product, the customer is entitled to a full refund **or** a free replacement, and does not need to cover return shipping costs.
- Partial refunds may be issued if only part of an order is returned, or if an item shows signs of use beyond normal inspection.
- Refunds for orders paid with store credit are issued back as store credit, not to a bank account or card.

## Cancellation Policy

- Orders can be cancelled free of charge **within 1 hour** of being placed, as long as the order has not yet moved to "PROCESSING" or "SHIPPED" status.
- Once an order status changes to "SHIPPED", it can no longer be cancelled. The customer must instead wait for delivery and initiate a return.
- Subscription plans can be cancelled at any time from the account settings page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle — no partial-period refunds are issued for subscriptions.
- To cancel an order, the customer can use the "Cancel Order" option in their order history, or contact support with the order ID.
- Custom or personalized orders (e.g., engraved or made-to-order items) cannot be cancelled once production has started.
- If a cancellation is approved after payment has been captured, the refund follows the standard Refund Policy timelines (5–7 business days).

## Shipping Policy

- Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days**; express shipping takes **1–2 business days**. Delivery estimates start from the date the order status changes to "SHIPPED", not the order date.
- Orders over **$50** qualify for free standard shipping. Orders under this threshold incur a flat shipping fee of **$5.99**.
- We currently ship to all 50 U.S. states and select international destinations. International orders may incur customs fees or import duties, which are the customer's responsibility.
- Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking number via email. Tracking updates can also be viewed in the "Order History" section of their account.
- We are not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier, customs processing, weather events, or incorrect shipping addresses provided at checkout.
- If a package is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it, they should contact support within **7 days** so we can file a claim with the carrier.
- Address changes can only be made before the order status changes to "SHIPPED". After that point, the shipping address cannot be modified.

## Returns & Exchanges Policy

- Customers have **30 days** from the delivery date to initiate a return or exchange.
- Items must be returned in their original, unused condition with all original tags and packaging.
- Return shipping is free if the return is due to a company error (wrong item, defective product); otherwise, the customer covers return shipping costs.
- Exchanges for a different size or color are processed as soon as the original item is received and inspected at our warehouse.
- Refunds for returned items are issued only after the returned item passes inspection, typically within 2–3 business days of receipt.

## Warranty Policy

- All physical products come with a **1-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects.
- The warranty does not cover damage from normal wear and tear, misuse, unauthorized repairs, or accidents.
- To file a warranty claim, the customer must provide proof of purchase (order ID or receipt) and a description or photo of the defect.
- Approved warranty claims result in a free repair or replacement, at the company's discretion. If neither is possible, a refund may be issued instead.

## Payment & Billing Policy

- We accept major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and store credit. Some regions also support buy-now-pay-later options at checkout.
- All prices are listed in USD unless otherwise stated at checkout for international customers.
- Failed or declined payments will place the order on hold for 24 hours before it is automatically cancelled if not resolved.
- Duplicate charges should be reported to support within 60 days; verified duplicate charges are refunded in full.
- Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address and applicable local tax laws.

## Privacy & Data Policy

- Customer data (name, email, order history) is used solely to fulfill orders, provide support, and improve our services.
- We do not sell customer personal information to third parties.
- Customers may request a copy of their stored data or request account/data deletion by contacting support.
- Payment information is processed securely through PCI-compliant third-party payment processors and is never stored on our own servers.

## Customer Support Policy

- Support is available via chat, email, and phone, **Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM (local time)**.
- Average first response time is under **2 hours** during business hours.
- For order-related questions, customers should always have their order ID ready to speed up resolution.
- Escalations for unresolved issues are handled by a senior support specialist within **24 hours** of the request.

## Account & Login Policy

- Customers can create an account using their email address or by signing in with Google/Apple SSO.
- Passwords must be at least **8 characters** and include a number and a special character.
- Forgotten passwords can be reset via the "Forgot Password" link on the login page; reset links expire after **1 hour** for security reasons.
- After **5 consecutive failed login attempts**, an account is temporarily locked for **15 minutes** to prevent unauthorized access.
- Customers can update their email, password, and shipping addresses at any time from the "Account Settings" page.
- For security, changing the email address on an account requires confirming the change via a verification link sent to the new email.
- Guest checkout is available and does not require creating an account, but order tracking and history are only saved to accounts that are logged in.
- Customers can permanently delete their account by contacting support; this also removes personal data in accordance with the Privacy & Data Policy, but past order records may be retained for legal/tax purposes.
- Suspicious login activity (e.g., login from a new device or location) triggers an email notification to the account owner.
- Accounts inactive for **24 months** may be archived; archived accounts can be reactivated by contacting support.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Log in to your account and go to "Order History," or use the tracking link sent in your shipping confirmation email.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Yes, but only before the order status changes to "SHIPPED." After that, the address cannot be modified.

**Q: How long does a refund take to process?**
A: Once approved, refunds take 5–7 business days to appear on your original payment method.

**Q: What if I received the wrong item or a damaged product?**
A: Contact support with your order ID and photos of the issue. You'll receive a full refund or free replacement, and you won't need to pay for return shipping.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: Yes, to select international destinations. Customs fees or import duties, if applicable, are the customer's responsibility.

**Q: How do I cancel a subscription?**
A: Go to "Account Settings" > "Subscriptions" and select "Cancel." The cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the emailed reset link, which is valid for 1 hour.

**Q: Can I return a final sale or digital item?**
A: No, items marked "Final Sale" and digital products are not eligible for refunds or returns.

**Q: My account is locked. How do I unlock it?**
A: Accounts lock temporarily for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact customer support to request account deletion. Note that some order records may be retained for legal or tax purposes.
