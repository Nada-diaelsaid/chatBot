# Product Catalog

This document contains the official product catalog used by the support assistant to answer customer questions about items we sell. Each section below is self-contained so it can be chunked and embedded independently for retrieval.

## Product Catalog Overview

- Our store specializes exclusively in **consumer electronics and their accessories**. We do not sell apparel, groceries, furniture, or other non-electronic goods.
- The catalog is organized into five categories: **Smartphones**, **Laptops & Tablets**, **Audio Devices**, **Wearables**, and **Accessories** (chargers, cables, cases, and power banks).
- All electronics listed are **brand new, sealed, and covered by the standard 1-year limited warranty** described in the Warranty Policy, unless otherwise noted on the product page.
- Prices listed below are in **USD** and do not include applicable sales tax, which is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address.
- Stock availability changes frequently. If a product is out of stock, customers can sign up for a back-in-stock email notification on the product page.
- Most accessories (cables, chargers, cases) are compatible across multiple device brands unless the listing specifies a single-brand compatibility (e.g., "MagSafe-compatible" or "USB-C only").
- Bundles that combine a device with one or more accessories (e.g., a phone with a case and charger) receive a **5% discount** automatically applied at checkout compared to buying items separately.
